KNOW THE CRAFT

What is Rolled Gold Jewellery?

✦

Understanding the technology and difference between normal imitation and genuine rolled gold.

Yova Rolled Gold
  • ✓ Real Gold Layer: Substantial micron layer of genuine gold mechanically bonded.
  • ✓ Long-Lasting Radiance: Does not easily tarnish or fade under proper care.
  • ✓ Authentic Weight & Feel: Heavier, solid brass base creates the true feel of gold.
  • ✓ Hypoallergenic: Safe for sensitive skin, nickel and lead free.
Ordinary Imitation / One-Gram
  • ✕ Microscopic flash plating that wears off after 2-3 uses.
  • ✕ Quickly turns dark or greenish with sweat and air exposure.
  • ✕ Very light, plastic feel and unnatural reddish tone.
  • ✕ Often causes itching or skin allergic reactions.
MAINTENANCE GUIDE

How to Care for Your Rolled Gold Pieces

✦
💧

Keep Away from Moisture

Remove before bathing, swimming, or rigorous workouts to keep the gold luster pristine.

🌸

Wear After Perfumes

Always apply hairspray, body lotions, and perfumes before putting on your jewellery.

📦

Store in Air-Tight Pouches

Store each piece separately in zip-lock bags or soft velvet boxes to prevent friction scratches.

✨

Gentle Cleaning

Wipe gently after wear with a soft dry cotton cloth before storing away.
